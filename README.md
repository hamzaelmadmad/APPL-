# AAPL_monte_carlo.ipynb
AAPL Monte Carlo Simulation & Quantitative AnalysisThis project utilizes Python to build a Geometric Brownian Motion (GBM) model and run a Monte Carlo simulation forecasting the 2-year price trajectory of Apple Inc. (AAPL). Designed to showcase rigorous quantitative analysis, the model evaluates historical stock performance, tests statistical assumptions, and generates probabilistic future price distributions.   Methodology & Statistical ValidationData Processing: Extracted and analyzed 20 years of historical daily trading data (5,027 days), splitting the dataset into a 4,525-day training period and a 502-day testing period.   Parameter Estimation: Calculated an annualized drift of 25.36% and an annualized volatility of 31.49% based on the training data.   Statistical Testing: Evaluated core GBM assumptions using the Kolmogorov-Smirnov test to check the normal distribution of returns and the Ljung-Box test to analyze autocorrelation.   Model Calibration: Backtested the median path against actual historical prices, achieving a Pearson R of 0.8158 and conducting confidence band calibration checks to measure real-world risk.   Simulation Results (100,000 Paths)Forecasted Growth: The simulation executed 100,000 future price paths, revealing an 81.8% probability that the AAPL stock price will increase from its current $336.13 over the 2-year horizon.   Price Targets: Identified an expected price of $553.94 and a median price of $502.25.   Risk Assessment: Established clear probability distributions, identifying a 5th percentile bear-case valuation of $243.75 and a 95th percentile bull-case valuation of $1,034.45.
